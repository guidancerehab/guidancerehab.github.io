What is usually going on

  • The room spins for a few seconds when you roll over in bed, lie down, or look up at a high shelf
  • It often shows up after a cold, a bump to the head, a long dental visit, or for no clear reason at all
  • It is more common as we get older, and it can come back, which is why I teach you what to watch for
  • It is not dangerous, but the fear of the next spin can make people stop moving, and that makes balance worse

How I treat it

  • Test which ear and which canal is involved, using the same position tests used in specialty clinics
  • Reposition the crystals with guided head and body movements (like the Epley maneuver), right there in the visit
  • Retest the same day to confirm it worked, and repeat if needed
  • If balance took a hit, add simple balance and gaze drills so you feel steady again

Every case is different. The real plan comes from looking at yours.

Common questions

How fast does BPPV treatment work?
Many people feel a big difference after the first repositioning, and most are done within 1 to 3 visits. Some cases take a little longer, especially if more than one canal is involved.
Is all dizziness BPPV?
No. Lightheadedness, constant dizziness, or dizziness with other symptoms can have other causes. I screen for that first. Sudden dizziness with a severe headache, slurred speech, double vision, face drooping or weakness needs the ER right away.
Can I fix it myself at home?
Sometimes, once you know which side and which canal it is. Doing the wrong maneuver can keep it going, so it is worth getting tested first. I can show you a home version for next time.
