What is usually going on

  • ACL reconstruction usually takes 9 to 12 months before a full return to sport
  • Meniscus repairs, shoulder repairs, hip scopes and joint replacements each have their own timeline
  • Most re injuries happen when people return before their strength and control are ready
  • Swelling, quad weakness and fear of the knee are normal early, and they all improve with the right plan

How I treat it

  • Work with your surgeon's protocol from day one
  • Early wins first: full extension, quad activation, walking normally
  • Progressive strength, jumping and running, with testing at each stage
  • Return to sport only when the numbers say you are ready

Every case is different. The real plan comes from looking at yours.
