What is usually going on

  • Stiff joints and tight muscles after long days at a desk, driving, or looking down at a phone
  • Stress and poor sleep, which make the muscles guard and stay tight
  • Headaches that start at the base of the skull and wrap around to the forehead
  • Tingling into the arm or hand, which means a nerve in the neck is irritated

How I treat it

  • Hands on treatment, including manipulation and dry needling when they fit
  • Deep neck and upper back strength, which is the part most people never train
  • Small changes to your workday and sleep setup that actually matter
  • A short daily routine you can do in a few minutes

Common questions

Is my posture the problem?
Partly at most. There is no single perfect posture. Staying in any position too long is the bigger issue, and a stronger neck tolerates it better.
Can you help with headaches?
Headaches that come from the neck usually respond well. I screen for the kinds that need a doctor first.
How soon will I feel better?
Most people feel easier movement after the first visit and steady improvement over 3 to 6 weeks.
